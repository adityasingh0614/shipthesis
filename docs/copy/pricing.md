## 3. Discovery Sprint: $750

> One week working through your idea with you. At the end, you have:
> - A written scope
> - A feature list, split into "version one" and "later"
> - User flows or wireframes
> - A stack plan
> - A fixed quote, with a timeline and milestones
>
> If you continue with us, the $750 is credited toward your build. If you don't, you keep everything from the sprint.

## 4. MVP Build: from $6,000

> Most first versions take 6–10 weeks. Your price depends on how many screens, types of user and outside services (like payments or video) your first version needs.

**Example:**
> An MVP from $6,000 is an iOS and Android app with sign-up and login, user profiles, one main feature (such as booking, ordering or a content feed), push notifications, a simple admin panel, and store submission. Payments, chat and third-party integrations add to the quote. The sprint tells you exactly how much.

## 5. How your price stays fixed

**Heading:** How your price stays fixed

> **The quote comes from the scope.** We price your build from the written scope the sprint produces. What's in the scope is in the price.
>
> **You pay as the work lands.** 30% at the start, 40% at the mid-build milestone, and 30% when we submit your app to the stores.
>
> **Changes have a clear rule.** Small tweaks are absorbed. Bigger additions are quoted before any work starts, or swapped for something of equal size. Nothing is added to your bill without a quote you've agreed to.

## 6. After launch

**Heading:** After launch

> **30 days of free fixes** for in-scope bugs, starting at launch.
>
> After that, **support plans from $300/month**:

| Plan | Price | Covers | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **App Care** | From $300/month | Your mobile app (iOS, Android or both) | Bug fixes, OS and library updates, store resubmissions, up to 4 hours of small changes |
| **Product Care** | From $550/month | App + backend | Everything in App Care, plus server and database monitoring, payment and login issues, up to 8 hours |
| **Full Care** | From $900/month | App + backend + admin panel or web app | Everything in Product Care, plus a reply within 4 working hours, up to 15 hours |

> **On every plan:** unused hours don't roll over, and new features are quoted separately. Need more than your plan covers? We'll quote the extra work before we start, or you can move up a plan.
>
> Requests come through a shared Slack channel or email. We reply within 1 business day, and the same day for critical issues.

## 7. FAQ

**How much does it cost to build an app?**
> Building an MVP app with us starts from $6,000, after a one-week Discovery Sprint for $750. The sprint fee is credited toward your build if you continue, and the sprint ends with a fixed quote for your exact scope.

**How do payments work?**
> You pay for the build in three parts: 30% at the start, 40% at the mid-build milestone, and 30% when we submit your app to the stores.

**What if I want to change something mid-build?**
> Small tweaks mid-build are absorbed into your quote. Bigger additions are quoted before any work starts, or swapped for something of equal size.

**What if I don't continue after the Discovery Sprint?**
> You keep everything the sprint produced: the written scope, the feature list, the user flows or wireframes, the stack plan and the quote.

**What does support cost after launch?**
> Support plans cost from $300/month, after 30 days of free fixes for in-scope bugs. App Care starts at $300, Product Care at $550 and Full Care at $900 a month.
