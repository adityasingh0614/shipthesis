## 3. Where app builds usually go wrong

**Heading:** Where app builds usually go wrong, and what we do about it

> **The scope keeps growing until the money runs out.**
> The Discovery Sprint puts the scope in writing before the build starts, and your quote is priced from it. See how changes are handled → *(/pricing)*
>
> **The app gets rejected at the last step.**
> Store submissions are our job, not yours.
>
> **The code only works with the person who wrote it.**
> Your code sits in your GitHub from day one, so another team can take it over any time. We use AI tools in our workflow, and we review and own every line of code we ship.
>
> **You find out about a delay a month too late.**
> A new build reaches your phone every week, against milestones agreed up front. If something slips, you'll see it that week.

## 4. What we won't do

**Heading:** What we won't do

> Saying no early saves us both time.
>
> - **Work for equity alone.** We're paid for the build.
> - **Take on a budget far below what the build needs.** If yours doesn't fit, we'll tell you on the call instead of cutting corners later.
> - **Work without one decision-maker.** We need one person who can say yes.
> - **Promise a fixed deadline when the scope can't move.** If the date is fixed, the scope has to be able to shrink to meet it.
>
> If one of these rules you out, it's better to know now.
