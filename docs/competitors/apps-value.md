## Offers & Packaging

**Homepage services:** UX/UI design, custom app development, App Store deployment.

**MVP offer** (/mvp-development-for-startups/):
- Fixed price against a written scope, with staged payments tied to deliverables.
- 8–12 weeks from kickoff to store submission.
- Phases:
  - Weeks 1–2: scope document and fixed-price agreement
  - Weeks 2–4: clickable prototype
  - Weeks 3–9: builds you can test every two weeks
  - Weeks 9–12: QA and store submission
- MVP is scoped to one core user flow.

**Pricing page tiers:**

## Strengths

- The most complete answer to founder fears in this set: price, timeline, ownership, and warranty are all stated.
- Public "from" pricing.
- A strong, complete case study with numbers and a timeline (Evesport).
- A named, physical location.

## Weaknesses

- Builds every two weeks (ours: every week).
- The homepage is generic. The strong content is buried on the MVP and pricing pages.
- The portfolio listing shows no information until you click in.
- Also offers React Native, so the Flutter focus is diluted.
- No evidence they ship their own products.
- Cost is missing from case studies.

## Implications for Us

- **They beat us on (today):**
  - Public price floors (ours is TBD).
  - A published phase-by-phase timeline.
  - A proven complete case study format.
  - Testimonials.
- **We beat them on:**
  - Weekly builds.
  - Our own live products (Poststeady, ChromaLayer).
  - Being honest about what we won't do.
  - A paid Discovery Sprint *before* the quote, so the fixed price is based on real discovery, not a proposal. **Unverified:** whether their weeks 1–2 play the same role.
- **Our ownership line is not unique any more.** Apps Value makes the same promise. Keep it, but don't lead with it as a differentiator.
