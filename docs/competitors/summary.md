## Landscape

Most of the field competes on speed claims and portfolio volume. Few competitors back those claims with terms a founder can check.

**Apps Value is the exception and the real threat.** It publishes price floors, an 8–12 week phase plan, ownership of repos and store accounts, and an in-scope warranty. Its founder-facing promise is close to ours.

**Synergy Labs** sells a similar "fixed budget, fast launch, boutique" story. Its proof is recognisable apps, but its own timeline numbers conflict and it says nothing about ownership.

**Flutter Your Way** and **What the Flutter** lean on headlines and authority. They say almost nothing about cost, time, or ownership.

## Synergy Labs — Overlap With Our Brand Brief

We asked specifically where Synergy's wording lands on the same themes as ours. The overlap is thematic. The phrasing differs, but a founder comparing both sites would hear a similar promise.

| Our brief theme | Synergy's claim (paraphrased; source) | Overlap | How we differentiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed cost** (fixed quote after a 1-week, $750 Discovery Sprint, credited toward the build) | A project-based cost they hold to the end; scope-based, not hourly (About, homepage) | **High** | Their quote comes from a 1–2 day proposal. Ours comes from a paid sprint that produces a real scope. Lead with *how the number is made*, not with "fixed". |
| **Timelines** (working build on your phone every week) | "10 weeks to launch" vs 6 months elsewhere; also "6 to 8 weeks" (homepage, About, blog) | **High** on theme, **low** on mechanism | They sell a headline number that their own project list contradicts (8–22 wks). We publish a typical range (6–10 weeks) and back it with weekly builds, not a headline promise. |
| **Code ownership** (your GitHub, your store and cloud accounts, from day one) | **Not stated** on any page checked | **None** with Synergy | This is a real gap versus Synergy. **But Apps Value makes nearly the same promise**, so it isn't unique across the field. Present it as a baseline we meet, not our headline. |
| **Founder focus** (for founders, by founders who ship their own products) | Team described as "founders, operators" and engineers; talk to a director of product, not sales (About) | **Medium** | They borrow founder identity for their staff. We have founder *proof*: live products anyone can download. Their audience is broader (startups, scale-ups, established companies); ours is founder-only. |
| **Small studio, judge us by work** ("judge us by live work, not headcount") | Boutique vs bureaucratic big firms (About, blog) | **Medium** | They frame "small" against big firms. We frame it against freelancers who ghost, which is the push factor in our brief. Different enemy, different buyer. |
| **Post-launch support** (30 days of free fixes, then support plans from $300/month) | 4 weeks free maintenance, then "flexible" support (homepage) | **Medium** | Our 30-day free-fix window matches theirs. We go further with three published plans (App Care, Product Care, Full Care), set hours and response times, where theirs is "flexible". |

**Net:** Differentiate on **how** we deliver certainty (paid sprint → evidence-based quote, weekly builds, our own live products), not on **what** we promise. Both "fixed cost" and "fast launch" are already claimed.

## Gaps We Can Beat Them On

1. **Case studies with a challenge, a result and a timeline, every time.** Only Apps Value's Evesport case study does this. Synergy's and Flutter Your Way's stop at features. Our case study template already requires it. *Condition:* we still need confirmed results for Access Yourself, the Safety training platform (EHS Guru) and Poststeady.
2. **Our own products as proof.** No competitor ships its own products. Poststeady (live) and ChromaLayer (in beta) are the proof no one else can show.
3. **Weekly builds.** Apps Value builds every two weeks. The others don't state a cadence.
4. **Saying what we won't do.** No competitor does this. It directly answers the "anti-persona" and trust fears in our brief.
5. **A quote grounded in paid discovery.** Synergy quotes from a 1–2 day proposal. Apps Value scopes in weeks 1–2, but it's unclear whether that's paid or binding. We can explain why our number is reliable.
6. **A single, consistent CTA.** What the Flutter splits three ways. Flutter Your Way is good here. Apps Value switches CTA wording between pages.
7. **Only shipped work in the portfolio.** Flutter Your Way shows "Coming Soon" projects. Our sitemap already excludes anything without permission or proof.

- **Portfolio size and recognisable names:** Synergy has multi-million-download apps. Apps Value has 14 projects. We launch with 3 case studies.
