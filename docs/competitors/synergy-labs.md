## Hero Headline & Main Promise

- **Headline:** "AI + Mobile App Development Agency"
- **Main promise:** Speed and budget certainty. They claim roughly 10 weeks to launch against 6 months for other agencies, and a project cost they stick to.

## Offers & Packaging

- **Services page:** Web apps, mobile apps, staff augmentation, custom software, marketing services. No packages described.
- **Engagement model** (About page): scope-based project cost rather than hourly, proposal in 1–2 days, and direct access to a "director of product" instead of salespeople.
- **Post-launch:** 4 weeks of free maintenance, then "flexible" ongoing support (not defined).
- **No named MVP package, sprint, or discovery phase** on the pages checked.

## Pricing & Timelines

- **Pricing:** No figures. The contact form's budget bands start at "Less than $30K" and run to "More than $250K". That signals a higher floor than Apps Value's $8k (an inference).
- **Timelines: stated, but they contradict each other:**
  - Homepage and About page: "10 weeks to launch".
  - Homepage and self-published blog: "typically launch apps within 6 to 8 weeks".
  - Per-project durations on the homepage: 8–22 weeks (Clapper 18, Peanut 22, Forbes 16, Open 8, DateRight 12, Slowly 8, Spendee 16). **Most of their own projects ran longer than their 10-week headline.**

## Strengths

- Big named portfolio with large user numbers.
- A clear boutique-vs-bureaucracy frame.
- Senior access with no sales layer.
- A defined post-launch window (4 weeks).
- US base, which is a selling point for US founders.

## Weaknesses

- Their timeline claims contradict each other (6–8, 10, and 8–22 weeks).
- Case studies lack outcomes, duration, and testimonials on the page.
- No ownership statement.
- Generic headline ("AI + Mobile").
- Broad service mix (staff augmentation, marketing) weakens a founder-MVP focus.
- The budget floor implies they're out of reach for many first-time founders (an inference).
