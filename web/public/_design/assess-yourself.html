01 · The brief

One app from "which exam?" to exam day

Assess Yourself · Case paperClient: AptellicTime allowed: 3-4 weeks
Q1.

What did Aptellic need?

ANS.One app where students preparing for UPSC, SSC and MPSC could find an exam, check its details and practise, instead of gathering it all from many different places.

Q2.

What made it hard?

ANS.A large question bank lived in Excel files with two layouts, Marathi numerals and image-based answers. And a timed paper of 60+ questions couldn't be lost when the phone closed the app.

Q3.

What shipped?

ANS.A Flutter app with exam discovery, a full test engine, instant analysis and a 15-day free trial, plus an upload that turns Excel files into ready-to-use tests.

05 · The hard parts

Three problems we had to get right

QuestionThe problemWhat we didWhy it matters to you
Q1 · Reliability

Tests that survive interruptions

The problem

A long timed paper can't start over because the phone closed the app.

What we did

Answers, review marks and elapsed time are saved on the phone after every tap.

Why it matters

Students never lose a paper they've half finished.

Q2 · Content

Messy spreadsheets in

The problem

Question files came in two layouts, with Marathi numerals and image options.

What we did

One importer reads both and flags bad rows without stopping the rest.

Why it matters

New exams go live from one upload, not weeks of retyping.

Q3 · Payments

One source of truth for plans

The problem

Trial and plan status could disagree between the phone and the server.

What we did

The server decides who's on a trial or a plan; the app only shows it.

Why it matters

Every student always sees the right plan.

06 · Judgement

What we chose not to build

  • Decision 01

    Recurring auto-pay, held back

    The renewal model wasn't decided yet, so we didn't hard-code a billing flow the client hadn't chosen.

  • Decision 02

    An importer that stops at row one

    Real files would fail it on the first bad row. Ours reports every bad row and keeps going.

Under the hood

Architecture and the decisions behind it.

EXPAND
  • Flutter app
  • 15 REST APIs

  • Firestore
  • Excel importer

  • Test state saved on the phone
  1. Trial rules live on the server. The app never decides who is on a trial.
  2. Row-level error reports on import. One bad row never blocks an upload.
  3. Content linked by IDs. Each screen fetches only the layer it needs.
  4. Test state saved after every tap. A closed app resumes where it stopped.
  5. Uploads handled below Firebase's default parser. Excel files arrive intact.
